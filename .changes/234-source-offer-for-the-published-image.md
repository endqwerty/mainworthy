The published container image and the executable say where to get the source of the Bun runtime's LGPL and MPL parts. `NOTICE`, which ships in the image and is served at `/docs/licence`, now carries a three-year written offer of that source, and [licences/SOURCE.md](../licences/SOURCE.md) names each part's source at the revision shipped and how to link a changed library again. No behaviour changes.
