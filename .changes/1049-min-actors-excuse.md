Planned runs that require two distinct passing actors stay pending when only one actor's disputed evidence is excused. An infrastructure or flaky answer cannot substitute for the second actor's pass. When an imported slot refers to a missing or non-exercise obligation, tallying counts it as missing and refreshing cannot mark it satisfied.
