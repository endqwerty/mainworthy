On a phone, Tab and accessibility tools reach only the pane shown. Opening a row moves focus to its detail, and closing the keys sheet returns focus to its opener. Actions offers Edit case once while reading a saved case and reserves editor commands for writable drafts. Automated phone checks cover accessibility-tree exposure, row activation, focus return, iOS and Android device viewports, rotation, reduced keyboard space, and axe audits.
