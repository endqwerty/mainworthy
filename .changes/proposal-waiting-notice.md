An edit waiting for a person is easier to notice. The queue writes "by an agent" on an agent's rows, counts proposals apart from what can wait, and says why a proposal is not in effect yet, from the policy clause that asks for the answer. A case with a proposal waiting says so above its title, with a link to the change. Proposals are titled by what they hold ("201 new cases"), and their accept and reject text says what each does; a new case's lines are marked `+`. Merge's text now says it proposes the merged version, which it always did. Each question in a reply carries `asked`, the clause and ask behind it; the queue's `counts` add `adopt:agent`; an adoption question's evidence adds `tally`.
