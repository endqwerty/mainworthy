## Your seed lens (candidate B)

Start from this whole-shape idea and push it as far as it goes; abandon it only if you can show it breaks, and say so:

**Change-centric.** The unit the product organises around is the *change* (commit, PR, release), the way Linear organises around the issue and CircleCI around the pipeline. The home screen is "what does this change need proven, and what has proven it". Cases are expectations that map to areas of the product; manual execution, CI results and agent sessions are three ways to produce evidence for an expectation *on a change*. Gates, coverage and triage are per change; the suite is the slow-moving backdrop. The agent's impact set and the human's manual run are the same query with a different actor.
